ExitPlan — Fake Call & Escape (Android)

An app that plays realistic fake calls, doorbells and alarms so the user has a
believable reason to leave an awkward situation. Framing is personal convenience
and feeling comfortable leaving — never harassment, deception, or surveillance,
and never an emergency service.
